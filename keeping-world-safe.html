Keeping the World Safe

Security Training Programme

"Safety is a basic human need. People with a sense of security and belonging are stabilized for learning, creating, innovating. A group of wonderfully cared for, confident individuals will generate great ideas."

— John Sweeney

Crime, terrorism and extremism have made the world a very unsafe place to be. The rich, wealthy, affluent and political leaders have become prime targets for criminal gangs and terrorist groups.

Taikan Africa recognizes the great need to arm VIP bodyguards with skills in combat training required to protect VIPs. Our programme incorporates the best of the best techniques in martial arts, to enhance combat training.

Professional Combat Training

Our trainees learn how to tackle attackers wielding knives, swords, guns or rungus (clubs). They will learn how to handle multiple attackers and stay alert to potential danger.

Training Components
  • Advanced martial arts combat techniques
  • Weapon defense training (knives, swords, guns, clubs)
  • Multiple attacker scenarios
  • Threat awareness and assessment
  • VIP protection protocols
Customized Security Solutions

We can also design, on need basis, training programmes for personnel of security firms, to add value to their services.

Who Benefits From This Training?
  • VIP bodyguards and close protection officers
  • Security firm personnel
  • Corporate security teams
  • Law enforcement professionals
  • Individuals seeking personal protection skills

Our comprehensive approach combines traditional martial arts wisdom with modern security protocols, creating a holistic training experience that prepares professionals to face real-world threats with confidence and competence.
