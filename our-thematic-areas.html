Mission

Our Thematic Areas: Empowering Children for Tomorrow

Building Character Through Martial Arts

"There is no keener revelation of a society's soul than the way it treats its children."

— Nelson Mandela, Former President of South Africa
Nurturing Future Champions

Taikan Africa aims to empower children as they go through the rigors of modern life. We give priority to Karate training. Being an Olympic Sport, our Academies strive to nurture and grow future champions who will, God willing, bring Glory and Honour to our Great Country Kenya. Under the watchful eye of our instructors, the children and youth who train at our various dojos, grow in stature.

Annual Children Leadership Camps

We shall soon establish annual Children Leadership Camps that will incorporate Karate, Football, Chess, Reading and Essential Life Skills.

Engaging with Nature

By teaching our children Karate and Aikido, Taikan Africa gives them an opportunity to engage with nature, outdoor and the wild. They get a chance to be children and to make informed choices based on God's menu. They get a chance to be responsible and authentic.

"Children are likely to live up to what you believe of them."

— Lady Bird Johnson, former First Lady of the United States

Karate and Aikido give children the essential ingredients of confidence and self-confidence, thus bringing the best out of them. We help children realise that the world is a place of joy and countless possibilities. They take delight in who they are and take charge of their own behaviour.

Building Character and Faith

"The greatest legacy one can pass to one's children and grandchildren is not money or the material things accumulated in one's life, but rather a legacy of character and faith."

— Billy Graham, Evangelist

Taikan Africa will inculcate in children the characteristics of both an Eagle and a Lion. Children learn to be independent minded and thinkers capable of relying on themselves. They also imbibe the spirit of teamwork driven by courage, honour and loyalty.

Teaching Resilience

"Each day of our lives we make deposits in the memory banks of our children."

— Charles R. Swindoll, Evangelical Pastor

At Taikan Africa, we teach children that pain is sweet. We remind them that when life throws lemons at them, they should grab them and turn them into sweetness. We teach them that failure is an essential part of life but we must internalize the lessons of failure. Through Karate and Aikido, we build in them stamina and resilience.

Building Strong Children

"It is easier to build strong children than to repair broken men."

— Frederick Douglass, abolitionist and Statesman

By indulging in Karate and Aikido, children learn that when they work with Nature, they become powerful creators. This realization instills in them a deep sense of worth and self-respect. With self-respect ingrained in them, they grow up respecting and valuing others.

Key Benefits of Our Programme
  • Confidence & Leadership: Children develop confidence, independence and self-respect, enhancing effective learning and turning them into great, dependable and compassionate leaders
  • Empowerment: We give children responsibilities, celebrate their achievements and accomplishments, and involve them in team and group decisions
  • Self-Defense: By learning Karate and Aikido, children will better protect themselves and their attackers
  • Academic Performance: Karate and Aikido improve children's overall performance in school and the world
  • Social Skills: Training helps in the critical development of children's social skills, making them more helpful and considerate human beings
  • Respect: Children gain a healthy self-respect and respect for others
  • Confidence & Self-Esteem: Martial arts inject in children an extra dose of confidence and self-esteem
  • Focus & Concentration: Training increases the power of concentration and focus essential for their stay in school
  • Physical Health: In a world ruined by sedentary life and lifestyle diseases, Karate and Aikido inculcate the habit of good exercise and good health. We teach about balanced diet and healthy foods
Core Values We Instill
  • Can Do Attitude: Every child must embrace this to live a fulfilled life
  • Perfection of Character: We teach children to seek perfection and be faithful
  • Integrity & Loyalty: Building trustworthy future leaders
  • Effort & Respect: Always putting effort in whatever task befalls them and respecting others
  • Self-Control: Enabling children to refrain from violent behavior

"Play is often talked about as if it were a relief from serious learning. But for children play is serious learning. Play is really the work of childhood."

— Fred Rogers, television personality

At Taikan Africa, we introduce children to the outdoor physical activities and games that their grandparents enjoyed in the wild—games that have become extinct. We talk to them and their parents on the dangers of keeping children indoors hooked to television and electronic gadgets.

"Every child comes with the message that God is not yet discouraged of men."

— Rabindranath Tagore, Bengali Polymath

Since children grow up rapidly, empowering them with Karate and Aikido will give them the necessary tools needed in life. Taikan Africa knows that once children go through our training programme, they become more helpful and considerate human beings—ready to take on the world with confidence, character, and compassion.
