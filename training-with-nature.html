Training with Nature

March 13, 2019

"Nature always wears the colours of the spirit."

— Ralph Waldo Emerson

At Taikan Africa, we train and live in harmony with nature. We encourage our students to always take time to stop, pause, listen to and enjoy nature. Take a deep breathe whenever you can. Thank the Almighty God for His amazing creations, relax and take in life in its abundance.

"If you truly love nature, you will find beauty everywhere."

— Vincent Van Gogh
The Virtues of Bushido and Environmental Conservation

In our endeavor to inculcate the virtues of Bushido in our students, we strive to teach them the delicate but fulfilling duty of conserving the environment, protecting animals, and encouraging the use of green energy. We champion the realization of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals:

UN Sustainable Development Goals
  • Goal 3: Good Health and Well Being
  • Goal 7: Affordable and Clean Energy
  • Goal 13: Climate Action
Launch at Nairobi's Arboretum

On Wednesday, March 13 2019, we launched our outdoor training at Nairobi's Arboretum. Sensei Piotr Borowski, taught us great techniques that continue to enrich our self-defense machine as we grow this Art of Peace.

Our programme was however disrupted by Covid19. We however, aim to turn the outdoor training into a movement where we indulge with students and clients in fitness and self-defense training that is fun-filled and health driven.

Join Our Outdoor Training Movement

We are seeking partners with whom we can have fun while:

  • Planting trees and conserving nature
  • Engaging in fun-filled fitness training
  • Learning self-defense in natural settings
  • Promoting health-driven outdoor activities
The Benefits of Training Outdoors
  • Enhanced connection with nature and the environment
  • Improved physical and mental well-being through fresh air and natural surroundings
  • Greater appreciation for environmental conservation
  • Unique training experience that enriches martial arts practice
  • Building community while protecting our natural heritage

"In every walk with nature, one receives far more than he seeks."

— John Muir

Join us on this exhilarating journey as we imbibe the wisdom of connecting with nature. Whether you're a seasoned martial artist or just beginning your wellness journey, training with nature offers a transformative experience that nourishes both body and soul.
